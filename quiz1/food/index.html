Local Food

Rendang

Rendang is one of the Indonesia's most delicious food, it has a really good taste, savory and spicy, and juicy meat. It consists of coconut milk and spices as its ingredients. Sometimes it is served with lots of Rendang Paste, which can be eaten together with rice or any savory food. Rendang is available mostly in Rumah Padang.

Nasi Goreng

Nasi Goreng is Indonesia's fried rice, it has a really good, savory taste, with grainy texture but not crunchy. It consists of a leftover white rice, garlic, egg, soy sauce, and sometimes with chicken as its ingredients. Nasi Goreng is available mostly anywhere, like street corners, restaurants, and more.

Sate Ayam

Sate Ayam is a skewed chicken with its spice and rub, it has a sweet, juicy and savory taste. It consists mainly of cut chickens, which can be the meat or the fat. It also rubbed with their unique sauce, like sweet soy sauce and spices, sometimes served with peanut sauce to make it more nutty and savory. Sate Ayam is available mostly anywhere in restaurants and street corners.
