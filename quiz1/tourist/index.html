Tourist Places

Borobudur Temple

A 9th-century Mahayana Buddhist temple in Magelang Regency, northwest of Yogyakarta. It has nine stacked platforms, six square and three circular, topped by a central dome. The temple has 2,672 relief panels and originally had 504 Buddha statues. It is the largest Buddhist temple in the world and the most-visited monument among Indonesia's tourist attractions.

Raja Ampat

An archipelago off the northwest tip of the Bird's Head Peninsula on New Guinea. It has over 1,500 small islands, cays and shoals around four main islands: Misool, Salawati, Batanta and Waigeo. It is part of the Coral Triangle, which has the richest marine biodiversity on Earth. Two bird-of-paradise species, the red and Wilson's, are endemic to Waigeo, Gam and Batanta.

Komodo National Park

A national park in the Lesser Sunda Islands, on the border between East and West Nusa Tenggara. It includes the three larger islands of Komodo, Padar and Rinca, plus 26 smaller ones. It was founded in 1980 to protect the Komodo dragon, the world's largest lizard, and later expanded to protect other species, including marine ones. It became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1991.
