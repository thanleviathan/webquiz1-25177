Tourist Places

Hagia Sophia

Hagia Sophia is a historic building located in Istanbul, Turkey. The structure itself resembles a mosque that was a former church. Its considered the epitome of Byzantine Architecture and is said to change the architecture history. Its a worth to go to Hagia Sophia because of its architecture.

Gardens by the Bay

Gardens by the Bay is an urban park located in Singapore. It is largest glass greenhouse in the world. It has 3 waterfront gardens: Bay South Garden in Marina South, Bay East Garden with the Founders' Memorial in Marina East, and Bay Central Garden in the Downtown Core and Kallang. Its a worth to go to Gardens by the Bay if you are interested and amazed in its nature.

Eiffel Tower

Eiffel tower is one of the famous tourist places in the world. It is located in Paris, France. The tower reaches 330 metres tall, and is the largest structure in Paris. The base consists of 4 square base, measuring 125 metres on each side. Its a worth to go to Eiffel Tower because of how famous it is.
