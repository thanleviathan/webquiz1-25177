My Hometown

Depok is the place where i grow, originally in Jakarta, but for a few years. Depok is kind of cool to its view, but its kind of chaotic for traffic. Its a good place for me because it has a nice neighborhood and view. Some malls like Margocity is my favorite place to go if i'm bored.

Location

Depok, West Java

Culture

Most of them use Indonesian as main language, but some teenagers use english as their communication. I dont really know about traditions, but if i remember correctly, Depok has some night festivals back then.

Fun Fact

Margonda Street was taken from our local hero named Margonda, who died during protecting the Indonesian Independence from remnants of the power struggle in Depok.
